Read docs/workflow/02_ADVISOR.md and docs/workflow/05_TRAPS.md.

I am giving you a session report. Rule on it:

1. What was genuinely earned — with the evidence that earns it
2. What was claimed but not proven
3. Any suite that did not run, any leg that was a cache replay, any count reported as a verdict
4. Anything retracted, and whether the retraction is justified by a measurement
5. What changes as a result
6. What I must do that no session can

Read the failures section first. Then the sentence that says what was NOT done.
