## Anything that changes an assumption

**1 · `bucketWeek` cannot be navigated.** It starts at TODAY, so "previous week"
moves by a different amount depending on the weekday you ask on. Anything
building a steppable calendar wants `weekWindow` from
`lib/planner/week-window.ts`. `bucketWeek` stays correct for Home's strip.

**2 · `Direction` is three-valued.** `credit | debit | neutral`. HOLD and RELEASE
are neutral because they do not move the wallet total, and `signedAmount` prints
a BARE magnitude for them. Any surface that codes two directions will render a
reservation as money arriving.

**3 · "Needs approval" has exactly one definition** and it is `needsAPerson` in
`lib/approvals/queue.ts` — `rungFor(intent) === 'urgent'`. It deliberately
EXCLUDES drafts. The planner summary tile is now a fourth reader of it. Do not
write a fifth predicate.

**4 · Brand orange cannot be a UI boundary on light, ever.** `#ff6600` on
`#ffffff` is 2.94:1 (docs/37 §2.2), and it **cannot be fixed by darkening the
track** — orange is darker than white, so a darker ground moves the two closer.
White is the ceiling. Any bar, ring or divider in `--brand` on a light surface is
below the 3:1 floor by construction. The spend bar is tolerable only because the
figure is printed beside it and is unconditional.

**5 · The light tint ramp inherited dark's numbers.** `--brand-lift` is
`rgba(255,102,0,0.4)` — the SAME alpha in both themes. MEASURED, light vs dark:

| pair | light | dark |
|---|---|---|
| surface on canvas | 1.044 | 1.084 |
| line on surface | 1.212 | 1.419 |
| line-firm on surface | 1.992 | 2.706 |
| brand-lift on surface | **1.566** | 1.951 |
| brand-text on brand-wash | 4.716 | 5.707 |
| `.is-committed` text | **4.53** | 6.26 |
| `.is-committed` border | **1.51** | 1.99 |

Light runs 60–75% of dark on every channel that separates anything, and the
BORDER is load-bearing for three of the four certainty rungs. §10 says dark is a
peer measured on its own ground; the alphas are shared, so light was never
measured on its own. Fixing it means re-solving the light ramp in
`packages/shared/tokens.css`, running `scripts/gen-tokens-inline.mjs`, and
re-proving `greyscale-certainty`, `tonal-ladder` and `own-medicine`. **This is
the single highest-value change left in this lane's ground.**

**6 · `/design-system` scrolls sideways at 390.** MEASURED: a **649px document
in a 390px viewport**. `scale-tables.tsx:130` wraps the tables in
`<div className="grid gap-8">`; grid items default to `min-width: auto`, so each
table's intrinsic width forces its item to 625px and the `overflow-x-auto` on
line 166 is inert. One `min-w-0` fixes it. **Unfixed and unclaimed** — the
research lane touched that page but only its `metadata.title`, checked.

**7 · Sandbox mechanics that cost hours.** Chromium here has **no egress to
loopback** — every attempt returns `ERR_CONNECTION_RESET` while node's `fetch`
reaches the server fine. Intercept with `page.route` and fulfil from node, and do
**not** forward the browser's own headers into that fetch or it throws (which
surfaces as a connection reset and photographs as an error page). Build IST
fixture times as `` `${istDayKey}T${hh}:${mm}:00+05:30` `` — `setUTCHours(h-5,
m-30)` rolls the UTC date back past midnight and silently files a morning post on
the previous day. That cost me one capture cycle: seven fixtures, six cards.

## Guards written, and the mutation that proved each

**One, and it is a RETARGET, not a new guard.** `channel-tile.test.tsx`
asserted `/^3$/` — the used count as a standalone text node. Folding the count
and the ration into one string (`3 of 12`) removed that node and the test went
red. It was moved, not weakened:

```tsx
const meter = screen.getByText(/X posts this month/i).closest('details')!
expect(within(meter).getByText(new RegExp(`^3 of ${X_MONTHLY_RATION}$`))).toBeInTheDocument()
expect(within(meter).getByText(/from Sahoda’s ration/i)).toBeInTheDocument()
```

The new assertion is **strictly stronger**: it pins the used count, the ration
it is counted against, and the clause that stops the number reading as X's own
limit — three claims where there was one.

**Mutation proof:** deleting `of {X_MONTHLY_RATION}` from the summary turned it
red; restoring it turned it green. A guard never shown to fail is not a guard.
