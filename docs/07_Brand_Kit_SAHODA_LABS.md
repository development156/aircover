## 1. Brand Core
**Name:** SAHODA LABS (wordmark ALL-CAPS; "Sahoda" in prose). The name carries the ally/companion spirit — the AI teammate, not the tool.
**Mark meaning (from the original kit, kept as canon):** two symmetrical geometric forms, mirrored and angled upward — the partnership between Sahoda Labs and the businesses it serves; neither shape is complete without the other, because our growth is intrinsically tied to yours.
**Mascot & AI persona:** **Sahoda** — the same identity as the product; constructed from the logo's own blade geometry (see 06 §5).
**Taglines:** Product — *"Your business, marketed. Every day. Automatically."* · Social — *"Dream big. Always. 🧡"* (from the original profile) · Persona — *"Hire Sahoda."*
**Personality:** Ally · Direct · Optimistic · Craft-proud.

## 3. The Blade Element System
The single blade is the brand's only decorative device — used with restraint (≤2 decorative blades per screen/asset):
corner accent (bleeding off-canvas, crop ≤25%) · section divider · 8px list bullet · **loading spinner** = two blades orbiting 180° apart · progress fill shaped by the blade curve · chart/highlight accent. Blades may scale and crop; they may not warp, gain strokes, or change hue.

## 5. Typography (from the kit's TEXT folder)
| Face | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Garet** | Display — H1–H3, hero numbers, poster headlines (Bold/Heavy) | Geometric sans matching the mark; **confirm license tier for commercial weights before production** |
| **Outfit** | Workhorse — all UI, body, generated customer sites | Google Fonts, SIL OFL, variable — zero licensing friction everywhere |
| **Helvetica** (per `helvetica-255.zip`) | Print/corporate alternate | Commercial (Monotype) license required; web fallback is Outfit → Inter → system-ui |
Rules: sentence case; tracking 0 body / −1% display; tabular lining numerals for credits & money; Hindi pairing = Noto Sans Devanagari; never mix Garet and Helvetica in one composition — Garet + Outfit is the pair.

## 6. Imagery & Illustration
Photography (per the original IG mock): **orange-duotone product and founder shots on Ink** — high contrast, confident crops; real Indian founders, storefronts, and products; no stock handshakes or laptop-glow clichés. UI screenshots framed in Ink browser chrome exactly like the poster. Illustration is built only from blade geometry + simple 2px strokes; no third-party illustration styles, no gradients-on-characters.

## 7. Motion
Blade-sweep transitions (250ms, `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)`) · hover lift 2px · loader = orbiting blades · celebration confetti (Orange 300/500 + Ink) reserved for real milestones only · everything honors `prefers-reduced-motion`. Motion says *precise and alive*, never busy.

## 8. Voice & Tone
Do: short sentences; concrete numbers ("Uses 6 credits", "Up 2.1× this week"); Sahoda speaks first-person as your teammate; optimism grounded in evidence. Don't: hype adjectives, guilt, fake urgency, or the word "guaranteed" anywhere near predictions. Hinglish is welcome in India-social; product UI ships proper EN + HI.
| Moment | Line |
|---|---|
| Approval card | "Your Tuesday post is ready. Predicted score 74. Ship it?" |
| Low credits | "12 credits left — this week's plan needs 60. Top up or trim the plan?" |
| Failure | "Instagram rejected the video size. I resized it and didn't charge you — approve the retry?" |
| Milestone | "First week fully published. That's the habit. 🧡" |
